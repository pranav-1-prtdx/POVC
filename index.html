I’m Pranav Bhatia, a Class 10 student in India. Most people online know me as VISHNU — that’s where the name POVC comes from.

I got into building because following tutorials got boring fast. Vishnu AI started as a rough idea I couldn’t actually execute yet, and I rebuilt it four times before it did what I wanted. That loop — build it badly, understand why, start over — is basically how I’ve learned everything.

Right now my work sits across AI systems, web development, and automation. I have no formal CS training, so I lean hard on documentation, trial and error, and shipping things that other people will actually use.

I’m still a student, so I’m not going to pretend I have years of industry experience. What I do have is a handful of real projects, one of them being built for an actual company, and the patience to keep rewriting something until it works.
